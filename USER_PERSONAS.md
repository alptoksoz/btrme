## 🎯 Primary Personas (Target Users)

### Persona 1: "Ayşe" - The Non-Technical Entrepreneur

**Demographics:**
- Age: 28
- Location: Istanbul, Turkey
- Job: Marketing Manager / Aspiring Founder
- Education: Business degree
- Tech Savvy: Low-Medium (uses Canva, Notion, Slack)

**Background:**
Ayşe has a full-time marketing job but has several app ideas she wants to validate. She's tired of paying $5,000+ to developers for MVPs that take months. She wants to test her ideas quickly without learning to code.

**Goals:**
- ✅ Launch 3 MVP apps in 3 months
- ✅ Validate business ideas before investing heavily
- ✅ Learn if her ideas have product-market fit
- ✅ Show investors a working prototype

**Pain Points:**
- ❌ Hiring developers is too expensive
- ❌ Learning to code takes too long
- ❌ Freelancers are unreliable
- ❌ NoCode tools are too complex (tried Bubble, gave up)
- ❌ Not sure if her ideas will work

**Motivations:**
- 💡 Wants to be an entrepreneur
- 💰 Limited budget ($500/month max)
- ⚡ Needs speed (time-to-market)
- 🎯 Wants to fail fast and iterate

**Technology Usage:**
- Daily: Instagram, Notion, Canva, ChatGPT
- Weekly: Figma (for mockups), Google Sheets
- Never: GitHub, VS Code, Terminal

**Quote:**
> "Fikrim var ama kod yazmasını bilmiyorum. Geliştirici tutmaya param da yok. Hızlıca test edip görmek istiyorum işe yarar mı diye."

**How BTRMe Helps:**
- ✅ No coding required (natural language)
- ✅ 2-minute app generation (instant gratification)
- ✅ Affordable ($19/mo vs $5,000 developer)
- ✅ Turkish language support (comfortable)
- ✅ Iterate fast (add features with new prompts)

**Success Criteria:**
- Created first app in <10 minutes
- Got live URL to share with friends
- Made changes without developer
- Converted to Pro after 2 apps

**Lifetime Value (LTV):** $228 (12 months × $19)

## 💬 User Interview Questions (For Validation)

### **Discovery Questions:**
1. When did you last need to build an app/tool?
2. What did you do? (hire dev, DIY, gave up?)
3. How long did it take?
4. How much did it cost?
5. What was most frustrating?

### **Solution Validation:**
6. If you could describe your app idea and get it working in 2 minutes, how valuable would that be? (1-10)
7. What would you pay for this monthly?
8. What features are must-have vs. nice-to-have?
9. Would you trust AI-generated code?
10. Would you share this with others?

### **Pricing Questions:**
11. How much do you currently spend on similar tools?
12. What's your monthly tech budget?
13. Would you pay per-app or subscription?
14. What makes a tool "expensive" vs "affordable"?
